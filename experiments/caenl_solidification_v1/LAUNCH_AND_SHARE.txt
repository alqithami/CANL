CÆNL public deployment workflow

Read ../../docs/PENDING_CAMPAIGNS.md before deployment. Required archived
inputs must exist at the protocol's expected locations and pass its hashes.
Never overwrite an active or resumable campaign's installation.

On a compatible new GPU host, from the repository root:
cp -R experiments/caenl_solidification_v1 "$HOME/CAENL_solidification_v1"
cd "$HOME/CAENL_solidification_v1"
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
python3 -B caenl_solidification_v1.py --self-test
python3 -B caenl_solidification_v1.py --start

Status: python3 -B "$HOME/CAENL_solidification_v1/caenl_solidification_v1.py" --status
Log: tail -n 40 -f /mnt/caenl/active/results/caenl-solidification-v1/run.log
Ctrl+C stops the log viewer only. The campaign is finite: 206 method runs.
Resume the unchanged installation with --start after its previous worker exits.

Review: /mnt/caenl/active/results/caenl-solidification-v1/review/CAENL_SOLIDIFICATION_REVIEW.zip
Verify the adjacent .sha256 file on Linux with sha256sum -c or macOS with
shasum -a 256 -c. Transfer both files through your configured SSH host.
Inspect RESULTS.json and all planned contrasts before interpreting findings.
A COMPLETE flag alone does not establish scientific success.
